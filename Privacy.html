The Shaken Circle

Privacy Policy

🔐 Overview

The Shaken Circle is a creative storytelling app for children aged 6–8. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information.

The app was created by Bianca De Vivo.

📡 Data Collection

The Shaken Circle does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

  • No account or registration required
  • No name, email, or contact data collected
  • No location tracking
  • No analytics or tracking tools
  • No advertising

🎨 User Content

Stories and drawings created inside the app remain stored locally on the device.

The developer does not have access to any user-generated content.

👶 Children's Privacy

The app is designed for children and does not knowingly collect any data from users of any age.

🧩 Third-Party Services

The app does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.
