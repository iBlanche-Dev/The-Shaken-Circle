Privacy Policy

The Shaken Circle

Swift Student Challenge 2026 Winner

Overview

The Shaken Circle is a creative storytelling app for children aged 6–8. Every experience begins with a shake that generates story elements.

Created by Bianca De Vivo.

Data Collection

The app does not collect, store, process, or share any personal data.

  • No account or registration required
  • No analytics or tracking
  • No location data
  • No advertising
  • No data transmitted to external servers

User Content

Stories and drawings remain stored locally on the device. The developer has no access to any user-generated content.

Children’s Privacy

The app is designed for children aged 6–8 and does not knowingly collect any personal information from users.

Third-Party Services

The app does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking tools.
